My philosophy

Three things I carry from psychology into research

Draft copy — placeholder titles, to be written properly together. Scroll down and watch them arrive one at a time.

Meeting people where they are

Good research adapts to the person in front of you — their language, their context, their pace — rather than asking them to adapt to the study.

Behaviour over opinion

What people say they'd do and what they actually do rarely match. Psychology trained me to watch for the gap, not just record the quote.

Empathy is a method, not a mood

Connection isn't a soft skill I bring alongside the research — it's the instrument. Trust is what gets someone to tell you the truth.
